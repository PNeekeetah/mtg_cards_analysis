Rating Guide

Game moments: Winning, Ahead, Balanced, Behind, Losing
S Game-breaking (4–5). Excels in multiple game moments. Often central to a deck’s strategy.
A Great (2–3). Strong support cards that work well with others.
B Solid (1). Immediate value in one key game moment.
C Fair (0–1). Average value, rarely game-changing.
D Poor (0). Situational or needs archetype support.
E Trash (0). Usually a liability.
Evaluation Factors
Mana cost – Expensive cards must justify their impact.
Mana complexity – Fewer color requirements are better.
Effect complexity – How much the board state changes.
Keywords – Quantity and usefulness of abilities.
Stats – Balance relative to mana cost.
